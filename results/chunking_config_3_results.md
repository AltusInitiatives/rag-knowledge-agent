[Q4] I'm getting error code TF-401, what does it mean?
--------------------------------------------------
Error code TF-401 means your session has expired or your account lacks permission to perform the action. To resolve this, log out and log back in to refresh your session. If the error persists after re-login, contact your workspace admin to verify your role and permission settings [Source: Help Center | Category: support_article].

[Q8] What happens when I exceed my storage limit?
--------------------------------------------------
When you exceed your storage limit, TechFlow sends email warnings at 80% and 95% of your storage limit. If you reach 100%, you can no longer upload new attachments until you free up space or upgrade your plan. However, existing files and all other features remain accessible. Upgrading takes effect immediately [Source: Pricing Page].

[Q9] How do I set up two-factor authentication?
--------------------------------------------------
To set up two-factor authentication (2FA), go to Profile > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. Click 'Enable 2FA'. Then, scan the QR code with an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password). After that, enter the 6-digit code to verify the setup. Make sure to save your backup codes in a secure location, as these will allow account recovery if you lose your authenticator device [Source: Help Center | Category: support_article].
